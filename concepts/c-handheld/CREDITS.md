# Credits

## Art

All pixel art in this concept was drawn for it and is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It lives as text sprites in `js/glyphs.js` and is drawn as inline SVG at whole art pixels:

- the 15 state glyphs on a 12 x 12 grid (call, approve, critical, moving, done, failed, blocked, waiting on its lead, scout, no word, running long, quiet, round 2, landed, continue),
- the machine pips (8 x 8) and the freshness lamp (6 x 6),
- the lookout bird (16 x 16) and its four moods (awake, alert, asleep, worried).

No art, character, logo, sound or font from any game or console is used. The screen door, dithers and hazard stripes are CSS gradients.

## Fonts

Departure Mono, Jersey 15 and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Versions, sources and checksums are in `fonts/README.md`; the licence texts are in `fonts/`.

## Data

`fixture.json` is a copy of the phase-1 synthetic fleet (`synthetic-fleet.json`). Every name, title and number in it is invented.
