# Credits for concept B (arcade cabinet)

## Art

All pixel art in this concept was drawn for it and is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- The sprite sheet in `sprites.js`: call diamond "!", approval "?", critical and machine triangles, capacity cell, moving, running long, done, failed, blocked wall, round marker, hourglass, magnifier, state-only dot, unknown dotted diamond, asleep "zZ", machine tower, health pips, the Player 1 figure, the landing star, the continue cursor and the no-signal screen.
- The starfield, drawn at run time from a fixed seed in `arcade.js`.
- The pixel frames, notches, plates, dithered range bars and fog pattern, all CSS.

The state glyph set follows the meanings proposed in the phase-1 research (`img/sketch-palettes.png`), redrawn here. No game sprites, characters, logos, names, sounds or fonts are used. "Player 1", "continue?", "high scores" and "stage select" are generic arcade vernacular.

## Fonts

See `fonts/README.md`: Departure Mono, Jersey 20, Jersey 15, DotGothic16 and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, all SIL OFL 1.1, self-hosted with their licence files.

## Data

`fixture.json` is an unmodified copy of the phase-2 synthetic fleet (`data/dashboard-design-research/synthetic-fleet.json`). Every name, title and number in it is invented.
